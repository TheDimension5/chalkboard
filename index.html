Pick who's reading

Five levels on every board: Kids (K to 5th), 8th grade, High school, College, and Max. The words, the readouts and the experiments all change. The choice is saved and shared through the link.

Do the missions

Every experiment has missions the page checks off by itself. The count at the top grows with the level, and progress is saved in your browser.

Read to me, and for teachers

On the two youngest levels a button reads each chapter aloud. Boards come with discussion questions for classrooms, starting with the physics board.
